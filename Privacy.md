Privacy Policy for Pizza Puppy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Pizza Puppy is developed by SoupFork Studios LLC.

Data collection

Pizza Puppy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require or use a user account. There is no server component, and no gameplay data is sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app requests vibration access for haptic feedback during gameplay. No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage permissions are used.

Advertising and analytics

Pizza Puppy contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Pizza Puppy does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Tim@SoupForkStudios.com.
